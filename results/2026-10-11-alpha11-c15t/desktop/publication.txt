Load results use 15 cold-browser pairs and three warmups per arm on an Apple M1 Pro with Chromium 153, CPU 1x, local production page assets and a 5-second observation window. The network preset was 12,500,000 bytes/s with 0 ms added latency. These are desktop lab measurements, not field percentile results. The confidence interval uses Bonferroni adjustment across 36 comparisons and two estimators. All 228 measured desktop attempts and 18 follow-up visits across six integrations passed.
